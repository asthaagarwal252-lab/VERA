# Product proposal

## Concepts considered

| Concept | Pitch | Privacy need | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ballot Window | Students vote once on a campus proposal without linking a vote to a person. | Strong, but requires governance configuration. | Not selected. |
| VERA | Students prove an access policy without surrendering their academic record. | Essential selective disclosure. | Selected. |
| Quiet Signal | Students submit anonymous wellbeing feedback with public aggregate trends. | Strong, but safer demo requires more moderation design. | Not selected. |

VERA won because the student immediately sees the value, the proof is explainable in a minute, selective disclosure is intrinsic, and a small MVP has a credible privacy model.

Gemini converts public policy language into a safe proof explanation. It is not an identity verifier and is never shown a credential.
